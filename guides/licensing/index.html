    Licensing and compliance¶

    The ForensiX framework itself (library, server, web console, deployment templates, scripts) is MIT-licensed (LICENSE). The models are licensed separately, as below.

    ForensiX includes only code and weights whose licenses allow commercial use. That doesn't mean every deployment is automatically fine. This page lists what to check. It's engineering guidance, not legal advice: have counsel review a commercial deployment.

    Three layers¶

    Layer What governs it Where to look
    Code The license in each model's directory forensix/<family>/<model>/LICENSE*
    Weights The weights' own license, or the repo's when the authors give none forensix.info(name).license; README Models tables
    Training data The dataset's terms, which may or may not carry over to the weights README footnote ‡ 
    for name in forensix.list_models():
    print(name, "|", forensix.info(name).license)

    Marks in the Models tables¶

    Mark Meaning What to do
    * (e.g. MIT*) The weights are linked from a permissively licensed repo but hosted elsewhere, with no separate license. Applies to xlsr-aasist, xlsr-aasist-df, avad, syncnet. Generally treated as covered by the repo license; flag it in your license review.
    † Built on OpenAI CLIP ViT-L/14 (MIT). OpenAI's model card lists "any deployed use case" as out of scope. That's an intended-use statement, not a license term. Note it in your risk assessment.
    ‡ Trained on data with research-only or non-commercial terms: FaceForensics++, DFDC, ForenSynths, LRS2, VGGFace2 and others. Whether those terms carry over to trained weights is legally unsettled. The main open question for commercial use. Get a legal opinion; prefer models you train yourself on permissive data where it matters.
    § stablesyncnet: CreativeML Open RAIL++-M Use-based restrictions apply; see below.

    stablesyncnet and use-based restrictions¶

    stablesyncnet's weights are under OpenRAIL++-M. Commercial use is allowed, but the license's use restrictions (paragraph 5 and Attachment A) apply, and you must pass them on to your users. Two matter for forensics. The weights may not be used: - for fully automated decision-making that adversely affects someone's legal rights; - to generate information for administration of justice, law enforcement, immigration or asylum processes.

    In those settings, use syncnet (MIT), which performs the same check. The full text is in forensix/audiovisual/stablesyncnet/LICENSE-WEIGHTS-OpenRAIL++-M.txt.

    Models you train¶

    avcons, avid, id-motion, physdf and person-detector are ForensiX code (no third-party restrictions beyond their frozen components), trained by you. The weights' terms then depend on your training data: - VoxCeleb2, LibriSpeech: CC BY 4.0. Attribution required; for VoxCeleb2, video copyright stays with the owners. - Your own customers' media: your contracts and privacy law decide. - Frozen components: SyncNet (MIT*), ECAPA-TDNN (Apache-2.0), DINOv2 (Apache-2.0), MediaPipe (Apache-2.0), ICT-FaceKit (MIT).

    Evaluation datasets¶

    Evaluating on a dataset is a use of it too. ForensiX's benchmarks use commercially usable data:

    Dataset License
    SDFVD CC BY 4.0
    ASVspoof 2019 LA ODC-By 1.0
    Deepfake-Eval-2024 CC BY-SA 4.0, evaluation only, gated
    ForensiX image eval set Open Images CC BY 2.0 photos + generators whose outputs allow commercial use

    FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF, DFDC, FakeAVCeleb, DeepSpeak and most other deepfake datasets have non-commercial or research-only terms.

    Biometric and privacy law¶

    Identity features process biometric data: - id-face: face embeddings. - id-motion: behavioural embeddings. - avid and the audio models: voice.

    Consent, retention limits and deletion rights may apply (GDPR, BIPA, CCPA and similar). Galleries (.npz) store embeddings, not images, but embeddings are still biometric data.

    Before shipping¶

    • [ ] List the exact models in production and their forensix.info(name).license.
    • [ ] Review the ‡ training-data question for each, with counsel.
    • [ ] If stablesyncnet is used: no automated adverse legal decisions, no justice or immigration use, and the restrictions passed on to users.
    • [ ] Attribution for CC BY data you trained or evaluated on.
    • [ ] Biometric consent and retention for identity features.
    • [ ] A human review step for consequential decisions.
